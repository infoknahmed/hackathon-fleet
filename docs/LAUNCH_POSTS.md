## C) Dev.to / Medium

**Title:**

> How I Built an AI-Powered Sign Language Interpreter in 24 Hours

**Subtitle:**

> VaakSetu turns any ₹10,000 phone into a two-way speech ↔ sign interpreter — free, offline-first, built with React, MediaPipe, WebLLM and a cloned voice.

**5-line summary:**

1. 7M Indians can't speak; AAC devices cost ₹5,00,000. VaakSetu is free and open source.
2. It works both ways: speech → animated signing avatar, and hand signs → spoken words.
3. 100+ SVG avatar poses, continuous ISL recognition, 5 Indian languages.
4. WebLLM and Whisper run fully on-device; voice clones are AES-GCM encrypted in the browser.
5. React + Vite + Express + Socket.IO + SQLite, deployed on Vercel + Render as an offline PWA.
